FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Ordering & Payment

What payment methods do you accept?+
We accept debit and credit cards through our secure checkout. We never ask for card details over WhatsApp, email or social media.
Can I change or cancel my order?+
Ready-to-wear orders can be amended within a few hours of placing them — contact us as soon as possible. Once a custom order enters production, changes may no longer be possible.
Do you offer wholesale or bulk pricing?+
Yes — get in touch through the form above with "Wholesale / Press" selected, and we'll follow up with details.

Delivery & Returns

How long does delivery take?+
Ready-to-wear orders arrive within 3–5 working days in the UK, and 7–10 working days across Europe. Custom orders vary by piece — we'll confirm a timeline with you directly.
What's your returns policy on customised items?+
Customised and made-to-order items generally can't be returned or exchanged unless they're faulty, damaged, or made differently from what was agreed. See our full Order Guarantee Policy for details.

Custom Orders

Can I use my own fabric for a bespoke piece?+
Yes — that's exactly what our Made to Order service is built for. Choose a style reference, upload your fabric, and we'll take it from there.
Do you offer click & collect?+
Yes, for made-to-order pieces — you'll choose your preferred collection point as part of the custom order process.

Fashion Academy & Resources

Do I need experience to join the Masterclass?+
Not at all — the Women's Fashion Masterclass is built to take complete beginners through to aspiring fashion entrepreneurs.
Are the digital guides downloaded automatically?+
Yes — once your payment is confirmed, your PDF guide downloads instantly. No account or waiting required.
