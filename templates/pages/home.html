About Us

A modern fashion and consulting brand rooted in elegance.

Oladiplenty Stylist Consult is a UK-based studio designing elegant women's fashion, offering bespoke tailoring, professional fashion education, and business consulting — all built around one belief: that style is a powerful expression of who you are.

Founded by a University of Westminster graduate with over seven years of industry experience, every piece we create and every programme we teach reflects a deep commitment to quality, creativity, and sustainable growth.
