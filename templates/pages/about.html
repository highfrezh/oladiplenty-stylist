Mrs Alimat Oladipupo Jinadu Sulaimon

Founder & Creative Director, Oladiplenty Stylist Consult

Oladiplenty Stylist Consult was born from a simple but powerful conviction: that the right garment, crafted with care and intention, can transform how a person feels. Elegance and modesty are more than design choices — they are a powerful expression of confidence, identity, and self-worth.

As a graduate of the University of Westminster with a BA (Hons) in Economics and Management, I wanted to combine structured business acumen with my creative passion for fashion design, pattern cutting, and garment construction. Over the past seven years of my career, I've focused on translating that mix into clothing that carries both exceptional quality and personal purpose.

From ready-to-wear collections that celebrate African heritage and contemporary British style, to bespoke tailoring where we bring your own fabric to life — every garment is built on high craftsmanship and direct collaboration. This same commitment applies to our Fashion Academy and consulting services, where we empower aspiring designers to build successful, sustainable businesses of their own.

This brand is a collaborative canvas for creativity and confidence, and I am proud to be building it here in the UK and for clients worldwide.
