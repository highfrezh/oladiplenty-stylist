Before You Order

Order Guarantee Policy

Read our full guarantee+

At Oladiplenty Stylist Consult, we want every customer to feel confident when placing an order with us. We guarantee that all orders will be carefully prepared, checked and packaged before dispatch, and will always aim to provide products that match the description, design, colour and size selected at the time of purchase.

Order Confirmation

Once your order has been successfully placed and payment has been confirmed, you will receive an order confirmation by email or through the contact details provided.

Customers are responsible for checking that all order details, including size, colour, delivery address and contact information, are correct.

Customers must ensure that all measurements and customisation details are accurate before production begins. Once production has started, changes may not be possible. Customised items cannot normally be returned or exchanged unless they are faulty, damaged or made differently from the agreed order.

Delivery Guarantee

We will aim to dispatch all orders within the estimated processing time shown on our website. Delivery dates are estimates and may be affected by courier delays, public holidays, weather conditions, customs checks or other circumstances outside our control.

If your order does not arrive within the expected delivery period, please contact us so that we can investigate with the delivery provider.

Incorrect Delivery Information

We cannot guarantee delivery where the customer provides an incorrect or incomplete delivery address. Any additional delivery costs caused by incorrect customer information may need to be paid by the customer.

Reporting an Order Problem

To report a problem with your order, please contact us with your full name, order date, a description of the issue, clear photographs or videos, and the date the order was received. We are committed to reviewing every genuine concern fairly and providing a suitable solution.
